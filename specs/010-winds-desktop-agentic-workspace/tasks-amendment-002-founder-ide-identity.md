## T142A — Establish the Winds IDE identity and left conversation dock

**Purpose**: make Winds feel like a premium native coding-agent IDE rather than a dashboard or centered chat client, while preserving all canonical authority boundaries.
### Required product anatomy

- a narrow Winds activity rail at the far left, using Winds-owned icons/geometry;
- **Chat is the default left tool window**; Projects is a sibling tool window, not the primary canvas;
- the left Chat tool window presents the exact selected canonical Session conversation/work stream and its truthful composer availability;
- the center becomes the operational Workbench for one or two exact Session identities, optimized for terminal/work surfaces rather than duplicating the primary chat transcript;
- the right side remains a contextual Inspector for Files, Changes, Evidence, Context, Artifacts, and Needs You;
- the bottom remains a compact status rail;
- selecting a Chat/Project/Session must preserve exact canonical identity and must never create broadcast or cross-session authority.

### Winds identity

Evolve `Quiet Current` into **Current Spectrum**: dark professional graphite surfaces with a restrained Winds-owned spectrum used for identity, focus, navigation, and agent activity—not for verification or semantic authority.

The visual energy may be informed by premium JetBrains/IntelliJ-class IDEs, but Winds must not reproduce JetBrains layout details, proprietary assets, logos, exact color sampling, typography, icons, or trade dress.
Required identity characteristics:

- a Winds-authored `Current Mark` based on flowing current/wind lines, not a vendor monogram or copied app tile;
- deep graphite/ink foundations with vivid violet, indigo/azure, magenta/coral accents used sparingly;
- status colors remain semantically separate from brand accents;
- compact IDE density, precise separators, small radii, strong typography hierarchy, and minimal elevation;
- no gradient text, glassmorphism, AI glow, oversized chat bubbles, dashboard card grids, decorative neon terminal styling, or copied competitor chrome;
- dark, light, high-contrast, reduced-motion, compact-density, 200%-scale, and narrow-layout behavior remain first-class.

### Authorized implementation paths

- `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`;
- `desktop/src/**`, `desktop/index.html`;
- `desktop/tests/**` and existing visual fixture manifests;
- `.github/workflows/t142a-winds-identity.yml`;

No root Rust source, Tauri Rust command surface, migration, package dependency, lockfile, or runtime/provider implementation is authorized by T142A.
### T142A acceptance

- Chat is visibly and structurally the default left tool window on wide/standard desktop layouts;
- Projects/Sessions remain directly reachable and exact-target selection remains deterministic;
